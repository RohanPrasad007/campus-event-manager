Welcome Students!

Welcome to the IIIT Vadodara Campus Event Manager, a platform designed to bring all college events together in one place. Here you can find information about upcoming technical, cultural, academic and sports events happening on campus.

Events play an important role in the overall growth of students. They provide opportunities to learn beyond the classroom, develop teamwork and communication skills, showcase talent and connect with fellow students.

Explore upcoming events, check their dates and venues, discover competitions and workshops, and register for the events that interest you.

About Events on Our Campus

Events at IIIT Vadodara are organized by different student clubs, departments and committees with support from faculty and the student council. They include coding hackathons, technical workshops, cultural programs, guest lectures, exhibitions and inter-hostel sports competitions.

Our aim is to make every event well-organized, engaging and memorable for the entire IIIT Vadodara community.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I register for a campus event?

You can register by navigating to the Event Registration section, entering your details (Name, College ID, Email, Course), and selecting your desired event from the dropdown menu.

Are external students allowed to participate in hackathons or workshops?

Certain events like the CodeStorm Hackathon and Web Development Workshop welcome inter-college participants. Please review the specific event rules or reach out via the Contact Us section for eligibility criteria.
